## Requirements with no Specific Deadline

To receive course credits, you will also be expected to:

* Collect 10 "E Credits" from experiments of other ExPra groups (these will be marked with "*E*" on [SONA](https://uni-frankfurt.sona-systems.com/)). For participation to be recorded, you will need to provide your "E" code.

* Actively participate in sessions and meetings

* Carry required tasks in time (e.g., reading literature, piloting experiment, collecting stimuli)

* Collect data for your project from fellow students, friends, family, flat mates, etc.

* Analyse your data using R

* Present your research results in the Presentations Session

* Present your scientific poster in the Poster Session

## The ExPra Report

The ExPra report is the final exam for the research project. The report is graded, and determines your final mark for the course.

### What should the report look like?

For full details on the requirements for the report, see the ExPra student guide:

<b> <i class="fa-solid fa-book"></i> <a href="https://jackedtaylor.github.io/expra-wise26/general/student-guide.html">Read the ExPra Guide for Students</a>  </b>

The idea of the ExPra report is that it should give you some practice in reporting research, similar to how experimental research is reported in journal articles. In terms of writing style, visualisation, and structure, real journal articles should also be very useful in getting an idea for what the ExPra report should look like.
